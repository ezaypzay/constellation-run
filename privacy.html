Constellation Run is a free iPhone game paid for by ads. You don't make an account, and we never ask for your name, email, contacts or photos. This page explains what data the game and its partners handle, and how to reach us.

What the game stores on your device

Your best score, stardust balance, unlocked ships and trails, daily missions, login streak and settings are saved only on your iPhone. We don't receive a copy. Deleting the game deletes this data, and it can't be recovered. A Remove Ads purchase is tied to your Apple ID and can be brought back with Restore Purchases in Settings or the shop.

Data collected by our partners

Google AdMob (ads)

AdMob shows the ads in Constellation Run, including optional reward ads (revive, double stardust, daily chest) and occasional ads between runs. To do this, Google's software may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), other device identifiers, your approximate location estimated from your IP address, which ads you see and tap, and crash and performance data. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and detect fraud. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

GameAnalytics (game analytics)

GameAnalytics helps us see how the game is played: how many runs people play, how far they get, which features they use, and whether they come back. It receives a device identifier, basic device information, and gameplay events. It doesn't receive your name or contact details. Read the GameAnalytics privacy policy.

Apple Game Center (leaderboards)

If you're signed in to Game Center, your best scores and daily challenge scores are posted to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname, where other players can see them. You can sign out or change who sees your activity in iPhone Settings → Game Center.

Apple (purchases)

The optional Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Apple. See Apple's privacy policy.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share data with anyone other than the partners above.

Tracking permission

After your first run, Constellation Run shows a short note and then Apple's prompt asking whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, AdMob may use your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you don't, you see the same number of ads, just less personalized ones. The game works exactly the same either way.

Reminders

After a few runs, the game asks whether you'd like a reminder when your free chest and daily reward are ready. If you say yes and allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your iPhone itself. No server is involved and nothing about them is sent to us. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications → Constellation Run.

Children

Constellation Run is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Constellation Run.
  • Reset your advertising identifier or limit ad personalization: see Apple's settings under Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Remove ads between runs: buy Remove Ads in the shop's NO ADS tab.
  • Turn off reminders, sound, music or vibration: open Settings on the title screen.
  • Delete game data on your phone: delete the app.
  • Ask about or delete analytics data: contact us at the address below. We'll pass the request to GameAnalytics and Google as needed.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Contact us to make a request.

Common questions

  • I bought Remove Ads on another phone, or reinstalled the game. Open Settings on the title screen (or the shop's NO ADS tab) and tap Restore Purchases.
  • My progress disappeared. Progress is saved only on the phone, so deleting the game resets it. We can't recover it.
  • An ad didn't give me my reward. Rewards are only given when an ad plays to the end. If it did and you still got nothing, email us with what happened.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top. Questions about privacy go to the email address above.
